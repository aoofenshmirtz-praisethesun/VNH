# Email to Orange City Water (OCW) — Draft

**To:** [OCW contact email]
**Subject:** Request for Zone-Level Water Quality Data — Viksit Nagpur Hackathon

---

Dear Orange City Water Team,

We are a team of students participating in the Viksit Nagpur Hackathon 2026 at VNIT Nagpur (17–18 August). Our project connects existing water quality measurements to the residents they affect, at the resolution they actually live at.

We note that OCW currently publishes a single city-wide water quality figure (99.6% "fit" as of May 2026). While this is reassuring at the city scale, it does not help a resident in, say, the Laxmi Nagar zone understand whether the water reaching their tap meets IS 10500 standards — because the measurement may have been taken at a different reservoir serving a different part of the city.

**We are writing to request:**

1. Water quality test results broken down by **supply zone or reservoir** (rather than city-wide aggregate)
2. The **dates** of these tests
3. The **parameters tested** (pH, TDS, residual chlorine, bacteriological, etc.)

This data would allow us to show residents which measurements are relevant to their specific supply zone — turning a city-wide statistic into something personally actionable.

Even if zone-level data is not available, any finer resolution than the current city-wide figure would be valuable. If the data exists in an internal format (spreadsheets, internal reports), we would be grateful for whatever can be shared.

**Why this matters:** Our project's core argument is that Nagpur's water IS measured — it just isn't measured where you are. zone-level data from OCW would transform this from a demonstration of the gap into a tool that actually bridges it.

We are happy to share our project and results with OCW. Our hackathon submission will be presented at VNIT on 18 August.

Thank you for your time and for the work OCW does in managing Nagpur's water supply.

Best regards,
[Team Name]
Viksit Nagpur Hackathon 2026
VNIT Nagpur
