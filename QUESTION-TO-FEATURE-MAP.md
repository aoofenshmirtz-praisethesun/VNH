## 🔴 One gap worth closing cheaply

*"Three million people, twelve sampled groundwater sources"* is your single best statistic — and **the product doesn't say it anywhere.**

Fix: a line under the title that counts what's on screen, e.g.
`395 measurements · 12 of them inside the city · 3 million residents`

Five minutes, and it puts your strongest sentence permanently on the screen instead of only in your mouth.
