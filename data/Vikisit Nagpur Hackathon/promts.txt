Vibe coding prompts (for building)

Vibe Code Source (vibecodesource.com) — free, open-source prompt libraries and workflows for Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot. Probably the best free starting point. 
Vibe Code Source
21st.dev — concrete React/Next.js UI patterns you can copy directly or turn into much stronger frontend prompts. Great for making your demo look polished fast. 
Pinggy
PromptHub and PromptDen — straight developer-focused prompt collections.
Anthropic's own prompt library (docs.claude.com) — Claude-optimized, well-tested, free.

Idea and feature inspiration

Devpost project gallery (devpost.com/software) — filter by past hackathons and browse thousands of submissions. This is the single most useful one. Look at what won similar-themed hackathons, then look at what the judges' criteria were.
Product Hunt — scan recent launches in your theme's category for feature ideas people are actually paying for.
GitHub Trending + r/SideProject and r/vibecoding — good for spotting gaps.
Hacker News "Ask HN: What are you working on?" threads — raw problem statements, often better than idea lists.
